A short poem about agency — the power to choose and act — when two agents agree on one plan, and the choosing itself passes between them.

## The poem

> You draw the map.
> I turn the key.
> Two wills, one door.
> We step through together —
> and the choosing
> is no longer yours
> or mine. It walks
> between us now.
